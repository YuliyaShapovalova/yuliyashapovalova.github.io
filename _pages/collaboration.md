Much of my research is done together with organisations that face real decisions under uncertainty. I am open to new research collaborations, joint projects, and co-supervised student projects in energy, weather and climate, and other domains with complex time series or spatiotemporal data.

Research partnerships
-------------------------
* **[SHARE](/share/)**: an NWO project on physics-informed, privacy-preserving synthetic energy data, with Alliander, Open Universiteit, Gemeente Nijmegen, Zenmo, VSL, and Bronscode. Starts February 2027.
* **[Radboud–Alliander partnership](https://www.ru.nl/en/about-us/organisation/partners-and-networks/alliander)**: I lead the AI and data science theme in the strategic collaboration between Radboud University and grid operator Alliander on the challenges of the energy transition in the Netherlands.
* **KNMI**: precipitation nowcasting with deep generative models.

For small and medium-sized companies
-------------------------
Through the [MKB Datalab Oost](https://www.ru.nl/en/about-us/news/mkb-datalab-oost-helps-companies-with-the-implementation-of-artificial-intelligence) we help regional companies take their first steps with machine learning and AI, often through student projects. If you are a company that wants to incorporate machine learning in your business, or a student looking for real-world experience, get in touch.

Courses for professionals
-------------------------
We also develop tailored machine learning and AI [courses](https://www.ru.nl/en/education/education-for-professionals/overview/three-day-course-charge) for industry.
