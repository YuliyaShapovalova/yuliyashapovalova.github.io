I am an assistant professor in the data science group at the [Institute for Computing and Information Sciences](https://www.ru.nl/icis/), Radboud University Nijmegen.

My research focuses on probabilistic machine learning, with particular interest in uncertainty quantification, time series, and spatiotemporal data. I develop methods for reliable prediction in complex real-world systems, with applications in energy systems and weather and climate. Methodologically, my work draws on Gaussian processes, Bayesian inference, state-space models, probabilistic deep learning, and deep generative models. I am particularly motivated by models that are scientifically rigorous and also make it into operational use, supporting real decisions under uncertainty.

Previously I was a postdoc with [prof. Tom Heskes](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~tomh/) at iCIS, and I hold a PhD from Maastricht University (*"Bayesian Inference in Multivariate Nonlinear State-Space Models"*), supervised by [dr. Michael Eichler](https://www.maastrichtuniversity.nl/nl/m.eichler). My work on Gaussian processes began in [prof. Magnus Rattray](https://www.research.manchester.ac.uk/portal/magnus.rattray.html)'s group in Manchester.
